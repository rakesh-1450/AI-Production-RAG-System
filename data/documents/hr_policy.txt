NIMBUS LABS HR POLICY (fictional sample company)

All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct during working hours.

Office timings are from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are entitled to 24 annual paid leaves per calendar year.

Remote work is allowed for up to three days per week with manager approval.

Emergency leave requests must be reported to HR before the start of the workday.

Employees must maintain confidentiality regarding company data and client information.

Use of unauthorized software or external storage devices is prohibited on company systems.

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year by team managers.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all internal platforms.

Employees must follow the password management policy and rotate credentials every 90 days.

Harassment, discrimination, or workplace misconduct will result in disciplinary action.

All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training every year.

Company laptops must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Suspicious emails and phishing attempts must be reported to the security team immediately.

Internal communication must be conducted using approved company tools only.

Serious violations of company policy may lead to suspension or termination.
