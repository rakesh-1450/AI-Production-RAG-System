NIMBUS LABS EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE (fictional sample company)

All new employees must complete onboarding within their first two weeks.

The IT department issues official company credentials on the first day.

Access to internal platforms is granted after identity verification.

New hires must configure multi-factor authentication during account setup.

Employees must read the HR policy and the security guidelines before receiving system access.

Engineering hires are assigned GitHub repositories and development environments.

All employees must install the approved security and productivity software.

Initial training covers project workflow, communication standards, and deployment procedures.

The daily stand-up meeting takes place every morning at 10:00 AM.

Employees submit a short weekly progress update to their team lead.

The engineering team follows Agile sprints that last two weeks.

Code reviews are mandatory before merging any change into the production branch.

Internal documentation is maintained in the company knowledge portal.

Support requests must be submitted through the internal ticketing system.

Every new employee is paired with a technical mentor for the first three months.
